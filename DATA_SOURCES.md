# Data sources

| Data | Source | Terms stated by the source |
|---|---|---|
| 79 room photos and curvedness ratings (`source: Vartanian 2024`) | Vartanian, O. et al. (2024). Neural dissociation between computational and perceived measures of curvature. *Scientific Reports* 14, 26529. Data: https://osf.io/h3uyg/ | No licence is set on the OSF project (checked 2026-09-29). |
| 112 room photos (`source: Coburn 2020`) | Coburn, A. et al. (2020). Psychological and neural responses to architectural interiors. *Cortex* 126, 217-241. Stimuli: https://osf.io/mc3aj/ | No licence is set on the OSF project (checked 2026-09-29). |
| Surface normals | Computed from the photos with Marigold-Normals v1.1 (Ke et al. 2025, `prs-eth/marigold-normals-v1-1`). | Derived from the photos above. |
| Python runtime | Pyodide, loaded from the jsDelivr CDN | MPL-2.0 |

Curvedness ratings are the per-image means used in the research project (191 rooms: the 79 Vartanian photos analysed and the 112 Coburn
photos, treated as one set).
