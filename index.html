    What the measure does

    Score = arc / (arc + corner). Surface normals turn sharply where two surfaces meet, so edges in the normal map trace the room's creases (wall–floor, wall–ceiling, furniture). Canny finds them in each of the three normal components; the edges are joined into chains. Each chain is smoothed and walked with a window of W px: a window is straight if its tangent turns less than the straight threshold, a corner if most of its turning happens in a quarter of the window, an arc if it turns steadily in one direction, and jagged otherwise. A round room should have many arc points and few corner points.

    The defaults are the measure called N in the project (Pearson r +0.667 with people's curvedness ratings over the 191 rooms). Ratings: mean curvedness ratings from Vartanian et al. (2024) and Coburn et al. (2020). See the repository README for sources.
